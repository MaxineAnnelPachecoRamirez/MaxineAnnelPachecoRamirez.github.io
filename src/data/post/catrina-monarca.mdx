La Catrina Monarca is a multimodal art performance by environmental artist **Geraldina Interiano Wise**, combining choreography, wearable abstract sculpture, and original classical music (_Las Campanas_). The piece draws on two symbols of Mexican culture, La Catrina and the Monarch butterfly, to represent metamorphosis, migration, and the cycle of life and death, and calls attention to Houston's role as a Monarch migration waystation. It premiered at the Zoom Art Gala in Paris.

## Mobile Brain-Body Imaging

The performance was recorded using **MoBI (Mobile Brain/Body Imaging)** and paired with AI-driven visualizations. This work was carried out under the NSF BRAIN Center (Award #2137255) and the AccelNet Movement, Music and Brain Health program (Award #2412731).
