Music in Medicine is a collaboration between the UH BRAIN Center and [MD Anderson Cancer Center's Music-in-Medicine Initiative](https://www.mdanderson.org/patients-family/diagnosis-treatment/patient-support/music-in-medicine-initiative.html). From **November 2023 to December 2025**, we recorded EEG from performers and audience members during live concerts at MD Anderson.
