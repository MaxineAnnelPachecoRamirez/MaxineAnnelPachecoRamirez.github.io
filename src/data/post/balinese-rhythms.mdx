In the summer of 2024, a mobile brain imaging setup traveled from the University of Houston to Bali, Indonesia. The goal was simple to describe and demanding to carry out: record brain activity from performers during live gamelan music and dance, in the place where these traditions are practiced, not in a laboratory.

## The Performance

On July 6, 2024, the *Neuroscience Gamelan and Dance Performance* took place at the Faculty of Medicine of Universitas Udayana in Denpasar. Musicians and researchers from both sides of the world came together for an event that was part concert, part live experiment.

<YouTube id="1Rf4MM3YTKM" />

## Research in the Field

Recording EEG outside the lab means working with what the setting gives you: a new venue, a different language, a live audience, and no second take. The team set up and ran mobile EEG recordings on site, working side by side with Balinese colleagues to capture high-quality data during the performance itself.
