In the summer of 2024, I traveled to **Bali, Indonesia** to collect EEG data during a live gamelan and dance performance, recording in its cultural setting rather than in the lab.

The *Neuroscience Gamelan and Dance Performance* took place on July 6, 2024, at the Faculty of Medicine of Universitas Udayana in Denpasar.

## Fieldwork

Using mobile EEG, our team recorded brain activity from performers during live gamelan music and dance. Taking the equipment out of the lab and into Bali was one of the most enriching experiences of my PhD.
