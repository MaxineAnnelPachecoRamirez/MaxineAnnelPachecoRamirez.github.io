This project uses EEG to record brain activity during live mariachi performance. I was part of the team responsible for EEG setup during these performances, preparing and configuring the caps on performers, ensuring signal quality, and supporting the live recording sessions.

## Background: Mariachi Sana Sana

This project is connected to **Mariachi Sana Sana**, a Houston-based initiative founded by Stephanie Horton and Gail Aflalo, both pre-health students at the University of Houston. Named after a beloved Mexican nursery rhyme, Mariachi Sana Sana brings mariachi performances to nursing homes and memory care facilities throughout the Houston area, offering a culturally grounded alternative to music therapy approaches built around Western classical repertoire. What started with three members has grown into a group of twelve high school and college students.
