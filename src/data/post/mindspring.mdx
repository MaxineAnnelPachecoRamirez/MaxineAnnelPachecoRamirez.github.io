MindSpring is a research project developed under the **NSF IUCRC BRAIN Center**, using **in-ear EEG** for mental health. Data collection and product development have been ongoing since **June 2024**. Within the project, I've designed an experimental protocol and led EEG data collection for the emotional component, work that will be used into my current doctoral research focus on **emotions**.

<CenteredImage src="~/assets/images/MindspringBooth.jpg" alt="MindSpring booth" />

## RED Labs Summer Accelerator

In summer 2024, the team was selected for the [RED Labs Summer Accelerator Program](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/centers/wce/programs/accelerator.php) at the University of Houston's Wolff Center for Entrepreneurship. RED Labs accepts roughly 10 teams each summer out of a competitive applicant pool. Over 12 weeks, the team completed a curriculum on startup fundamentals, including customer discovery and legal and IP office hours, alongside one-on-one mentorship. The program culminated in the Bayou Startup Showcase.

## Neurable Research Kit

MindSpring was selected to receive a **Neurable research kit**, providing access to [Neurable's](https://www.neurable.com/) EEG technology integrated into everyday wearables for testing and validation.

## Industry Partnership

MindSpring has been developed in collaboration with **Ferguson Control Systems**.
