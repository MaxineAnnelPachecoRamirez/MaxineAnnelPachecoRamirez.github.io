Diabelli 200 marks the first time a conductor wore full-scale neuroimaging equipment during a live performance. Both the conductor and pianist wore 32-channel EEG caps while performing Anthony Brandt's piece inspired by Beethoven's _Diabelli Variations_, recording neural activity during musical interpretation. I was part of the technical team responsible for the EEG data capture during the performance.

## Recognition

The analysis of Diabelli 200 data by Shepherd School violinist Nanki Chugh won **first place for graduate oral presentation in neuroscience** at the 2024 Emerging Researchers National Conference in STEM.

## Partner Institutions

- University of Houston, BRAIN Center
- Rice University, Shepherd School of Music

## Timeline

Data collection took place from **October 17 to October 24, 2024**. We are currently working on the **data descriptor** for publication.
